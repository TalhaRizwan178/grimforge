# The Wrong Kind of Chemistry

**Genre:** Crime / Comedy / Romance
**Custom Genre:** Romantic Chaos
**Tone:** Witty / Romantic / Light-hearted

## Plot

Nadia Voss is a forensic chemist who is very good at her job and very bad at her personal life. She has been engaged twice, stood up once, and is currently on a strict self-imposed dating ban that she has maintained for eleven months and four days. She works clean, thinks clean, and keeps her lab immaculate. She does not, under any circumstances, get involved in things that are messy.

Then she accidentally witnesses a mob drop-off through her apartment window, photographs it without thinking, and spends the next forty-eight hours being tailed by a man who is entirely too attractive to be following her for legitimate reasons.

His name is Callum Reeve. He is not a cop — he is a private investigator hired by the city's second-largest crime family to recover a stolen shipment before the first-largest crime family finds out it is missing. He expected this job to be straightforward. He did not expect the only witness to be a forensic chemist who responds to being followed by leaving a passive-aggressive note on his car and reporting him to three separate city departments.

They do not like each other immediately. This is mutual, vocal, and completely unconvincing to everyone around them.

Forced into reluctant cooperation — she has the photographs, he has the connections, and both of them are now too deep to walk away — they navigate a city where every lead is a bad idea and every bad idea somehow works. Nadia applies forensic logic to situations that have no business being logical. Callum improvises everything and refuses to admit it. Together they are chaotic, bickering, and increasingly unable to pretend that the tension between them is purely professional.

Standing between them and getting out alive: two crime families, one very confused police lieutenant, a witness protection case gone sideways, and the growing, inconvenient, poorly-timed realisation that they are falling for each other in the middle of the worst possible circumstances.

The city is dangerous. The case is a disaster. The timing is terrible. Neither of them planned for this — but then again, neither of them planned for any of it.
